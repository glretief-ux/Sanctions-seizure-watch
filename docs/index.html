Goods screening: dual-use, military and sanctioned goods

type or paste the product description or HS code from a bill of lading, manifest, invoice or packing list · one product per line · checks three things: (1) sanctioned and embargoed goods by regime (EU, UN, US, UK), (2) dual-use and military control families, (3) the Common High Priority Items list · a result is a lead to check; no result is not clearance, because the legal test is the technical specification and the current annex text

Check the official sources

  • EU Dual-Use Regulation 2021/821 and Annex I (the legal control list for the EU)
  • EU list of Common High Priority Items and US BIS Common High Priority Items List: HS codes of items found in weapons used against Ukraine
  • Wassenaar Arrangement control lists · US Consolidated Screening List
  • Your national customs tariff and control list correlation table: the HS code on a manifest can be wrong or vague, so always compare the goods and data sheet with the control list entry. See the handbook, chapter 7.

What this tool does

  • Every day it searches open-source news (Google News editions in many languages, GDELT, and the official customs, coast guard and ministry newsrooms listed in config.yaml) for reports where goods, vessels or assets were seized and sanctions were involved: UN, EU, US (OFAC), UK and other regimes, arms embargoes, export controls on dual-use goods, the shadow fleet, and sanctioned owners.
  • Facts are read by rules, with no AI service and no cost: what was seized, which regime, who seized it, vessel name and IMO number, flag, origin, transit, destination, quantity and stated value, and the sanctions-evasion signs the article names.
  • Only extracted facts and the article link are stored, not the article text. Open the link to check every item.

Limits

  • News coverage favours large and unusual seizures and some countries and languages; the absence of a report is not the absence of seizures.
  • A sanctions cue in an article does not prove a seizure was made under that regime. The evasion signs are a pointer for a closer look, never a finding about a person, company or country.
  • Transit hubs are places where diverted goods are often reported. They are leads, not accusations against a state.
